# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Designers and creatives who need editable design files (PSD, AI, Figma, Sketch, After Effects, mockups) for their projects. They work under commercial-use licenses and are either individual buyers or subscribers seeking ongoing access to a growing catalog.

## Product Purpose

A Spanish-language marketplace that sells editable design files through individual purchases and recurring subscriptions. Success: designers find the resources they need quickly, trust the quality, and return for more.

## Brand Personality

Creativo, Moderno, Bold — confident and energetic without being overwhelming. The interface should feel like a curated design library, not a generic store.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS with cream/sand/paper background — the AI default of 2026
- Stock template feel — looks like a free Bootstrap theme
- Canva dashboard — too colorful, overwhelming
- Anything that feels "AI-generated" — no warm-tinted off-whites, no identical card grids, no eyebrow tropes

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — product imagery does the heavy lifting, not copy
2. **Curated over crowded** — whitespace and hierarchy over cramming everything in
3. **Bold but controlled** — strong visual accents with restraint, not decorative excess
4. **Spanish-first** — all copy, labels, and UX copy in Spanish
5. **Creator confidence** — the interface should feel professional enough that designers trust the product quality

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA target (4.5:1 contrast for body text)
- Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Touch targets ≥ 44px on all interactive elements
- Skip-to-content link for keyboard navigation
- ARIA labels on interactive elements
